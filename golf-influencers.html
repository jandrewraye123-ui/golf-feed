Golf influencers worth following on Instagram and TikTok

A lot of fans find golf creators through short clips before they ever watch a full YouTube video. These are golf influencers on Instagram and TikTok who stand out.

Grace Charis

TikTok and YouTube

Biggest on TikTok

Grace is one of the most followed golf accounts on TikTok, with around 3 million followers there according to FeedSpot. Her YouTube channel has grown fast too, reaching about 1.49 million subscribers with only a few dozen videos.

Paige Spiranac

Instagram, YouTube, and more

Golf influencer pioneer

Paige is often called the original golf influencer. Her content covers course vlogs, golf culture, and commentary, and she has crossed over into creator collaborations, including an episode of Bryson DeChambeau’s Break 50.

Good Good Girls

YouTube and social

Women in creator golf

Good Good’s female-focused brand is led by Alexis Miestowski and Marissa Wenzler. The two were also named co-general managers of the Dallas Horsemen in the Grass League this fall.

Twin Tour Golf

Instagram

Instagram favorite

With more than 370,000 followers, Twin Tour Golf is one of the larger golf accounts on Instagram according to HypeAuditor’s 2026 rankings.

Claire (@caddie_issues)

Instagram

High engagement

Claire’s account sits near the top of HypeAuditor’s 2026 golf rankings, with close to 300,000 followers and one of the stronger engagement rates in the category.

Why short-form video works so well for golf

Golf is full of moments that fit perfectly in a 15-second clip: a hole-in-one, a wild trick shot, or a reaction to a terrible miss. Short videos let creators reach people who would never sit through a full round, and those viewers often follow the creator to YouTube for longer content.

For fans, the easiest move is to follow your favorite YouTube creators on Instagram and TikTok too. That is usually where they post highlights, announce new series, and share behind-the-scenes moments first.
