What’s happening in creator golf

Good Good is rebuilding after its Callaway ad

A co-branded driver ad from Good Good and Callaway was pulled in late August after heavy backlash. Callaway ended the partnership, several retailers pulled the merchandise, and Good Good’s CEO and president left the company. Read the full breakdown on our brand deals page.

Break 50 keeps landing huge guests

Bryson DeChambeau’s Break 50 series has brought in names like Steph Curry and Carlos Alcaraz, and it remains one of the most watched shows in golf YouTube. See why on our golf YouTubers page.

Good Good Girls step into team management

Alexis Miestowski and Marissa Wenzler, who lead Good Good’s female-focused channel, were named co-general managers of the Dallas Horsemen in the Grass League in late September.
